## What's new

Every time you sign in, we email you when it happened, from which IP address, from which country and from which browser.

## How to use it

You don't need to do anything. If it wasn't you, follow the link in the email and change your password.

## How it works

The email is sent after signing in with email and password. If you sign in again from the same device within 10 minutes, you won't get a second email.
