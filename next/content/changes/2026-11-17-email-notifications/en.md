## What's new

You can now get emails: teachers get an evening reminder when their streak is at risk or they have been away from the studio for a while, and everyone can get project news. Everything stays off until you turn it on.

## How to use it

1. Open your profile and find the "Notifications" section.
2. Turn on "Studio reminders" or "Project news".
3. To unsubscribe, click the link at the bottom of any email and confirm. You can resubscribe on the same page.

## How it works

Reminders come at most once a day, around 19:00 Irkutsk time. Service emails (password reset, new sign-in alerts) always arrive and cannot be turned off.
