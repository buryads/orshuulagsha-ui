## What's new

We rebuilt the site: a new design, faster and friendlier on phones.

- A translator with a full Buryat keyboard.
- A "Quiz" page to practise words.
- An "Open in dictionary" button next to a translation.

## How to use

1. Type a word or text into the translator on the home page.
2. For Buryat letters, use the on-screen keyboard under the input.
3. To practise, open "Quiz" in the menu.

## How it works

The translator looks the word up in the dictionary. Translations can be inaccurate; 100% correctness is not guaranteed.
