## What's new

The "Help the project" page now explains how we use your contribution: your voice, words and lesson corrections are open to everyone under the CC BY 4.0 license. We do not publish your contacts. There is a new "Policy and license" page, and the application form requires agreeing to the terms.

## How to use

1. Open "Help the project" at the bottom of the site.
2. Read the "How we use your contribution" block.
3. Tick the agreement and send your application.

## How it works

Attribution is "buryads.com and contributors". You can ask us to delete your data and recordings.
