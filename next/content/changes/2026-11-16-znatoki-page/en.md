## What's new

A new page where we look for Buryat language experts. We don't ask for money, we ask for your voice and knowledge: record phrases, check lessons, share words and examples. The page has a poster and a ready-made text for a post.

## How to use

1. Open "Help the project" at the bottom of the site.
2. Download the image or copy the text and share it with friends.
3. Fill in the form at the bottom and leave your Telegram — we will write to you.

## How it works

Helpers will be added to the wall of honor. A phone or a computer and 10 minutes a day is enough.
