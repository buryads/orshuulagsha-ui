## What's new

There is now a profile page: name, email, MCP access, password change and a Log out button.

## How to use

Press Log out in the header. On a phone the button is in the menu, and it is also on the profile page (the avatar in the header).

## How it works

Logging out ends the session on this device. Changing the password emails you a reset link.
