## What's new

Through the MCP connection you can suggest your own translation of a word for the Buryads dictionary.

## How to use

Ask the AI assistant connected to Buryads via MCP: "suggest a translation: house → гэр".

You can add a comment, for example a source or context.

## How it works

The suggestion is saved and reviewed manually. It reaches the dictionary only after review.
