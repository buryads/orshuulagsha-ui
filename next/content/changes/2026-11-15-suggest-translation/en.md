## What's new

Through a connected assistant you can suggest your own translation of a word for the Buryads dictionary.

## How to use

Ask the AI assistant connected to Buryads (the "AI" page): "suggest a translation: house → гэр".

You can add a comment, for example a source or context.

## How it works

The suggestion is saved and reviewed. It reaches the dictionary only after review.
