## What's new

If a word is missing from the dictionary, the search result now has an "Ask an expert" button. Experts see the most wanted words and suggest a translation, and after a check the word appears in the dictionary.

## How to use it

1. Look the word up in the translator. If it is missing, press "Ask an expert" (you need to sign in).
2. If the word is already requested, press "Join".
3. Follow the status in the profile menu, under "My requests".
4. Want an email when the word appears? Tick "Send project news: new words and phrase of the day". You can unsubscribe from any email.

## How it works

A word enters the dictionary after two experts confirm the translation and a moderator checks it. You can send up to 10 requests a day.
