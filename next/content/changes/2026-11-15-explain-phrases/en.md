## What's new

A new "Explain" page: paste a phrase in Buryat, Russian or English to see its words, their forms and what the phrase means.

## How to use

Sign in, open "Explain", pick the phrase language, enter the text (up to 300 characters) and press "Break down".

For a Russian or English phrase, AI first builds the Buryat version and then breaks it down word by word.

## How it works

A morphological analyzer breaks the words down, meanings come from the Buryads dictionary, and the explanation is written by AI. AI answers can be wrong, and the feature is in beta. Words marked "check" were not confirmed by the analyzer.
