## What's new

You can connect the buryads dictionary to Claude and other AI assistants. The assistant can:

- translate words and texts ru↔bur;
- show a word card and a link to its pronunciation;
- find examples in the corpus;
- work with your word packs and run a quiz;
- suggest the word of the day;
- show your history and favourites, and add or remove favourite words;
- create word packs;
- check Buryat text: the dictionary plus AI remarks (marked as AI).

## How to use

1. Open the "AI" page and copy the connection command.
2. Create a personal token in your profile, in the MCP section.
3. Paste the token into your assistant's settings and ask, for example: "translate into Buryat: good afternoon".

## How it works

The assistant talks to the dictionary on your behalf using your personal token. AI translations and remarks can be wrong, so such answers are marked as AI.
