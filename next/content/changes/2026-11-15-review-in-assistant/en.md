## What's new

The assistant (Claude and others) can now run a review of words from your packs with you.

- It shows words that are due for a refresh, plus new ones.
- Hard words come back more often, easy ones less often.
- Your progress is saved in your account.

## How to use

1. Connect the dictionary to your assistant (the "AI" page).
2. Ask: "review today's words".
3. After each word, rate from 0 to 5 how easily you recalled it.

## How it works

Based on your rating, the system decides when to show the word again. The harder the word, the sooner it returns. The assistant can be wrong, so double-check anything important.
