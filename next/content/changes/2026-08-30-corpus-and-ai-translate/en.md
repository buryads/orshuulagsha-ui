## What's new

- A "Corpus" page: search for examples in Buryat texts.
- The main translator has an AI translation switch: it adds a note on the word and examples.

## How to use

1. For the corpus, open "Corpus" in the menu and type a word.
2. For AI translation, turn on the switch in the translator and enter your text.

## How it works

The corpus searches collected texts and shows the word in context. AI translation is generated by AI, so it can be inaccurate.
