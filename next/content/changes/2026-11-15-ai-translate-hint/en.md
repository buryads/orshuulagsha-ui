## What's new

Guests on the home page now see a hint: after signing in you can turn on AI translation.

## How to use

1. Sign in using the link in the hint.
2. Turn on the AI translation switch in the translator.

## How it works

AI translation handles phrases and whole sentences better. It can be wrong, so AI translations are marked.
