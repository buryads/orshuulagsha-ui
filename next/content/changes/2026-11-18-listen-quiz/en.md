## What's new

The quiz has a new "Listen" tab: hear live recordings by speakers and pick the word you heard. If you cannot hear a recording, press "Can't hear". Speakers can see how many times their voice was recognised and re-record difficult recordings.

## How to use it

1. Open "Quiz" and switch to the "Listen" tab.
2. Press "Listen" and pick the word.
3. See your result at the end of the series.

## How it works

A series has 10 recordings. Answers are stored anonymously and help improve the recordings.
