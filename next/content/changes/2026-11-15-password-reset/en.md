## What's new

If you forgot your password, you can set a new one using a link sent to your email.

## How to use

1. On the sign-in page, click "Forgot password?" and enter your email.
2. Open the email and follow the link.
3. Choose a new password and sign in with it.

## How it works

The link is valid for 60 minutes and works only once. If it has expired, request a new one.
