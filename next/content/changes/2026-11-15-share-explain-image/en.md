## What's new

You can send the result of the "Explain" page as an image: the phrase, its meaning, the words and notes on one sheet.

## How to use

1. Break down a phrase.
2. On a phone, tap "Share" and pick an app.
3. On a computer, press "Copy image" and paste it into a chat, or press "Download".

## How it works

The image is built right in your browser. The explanation on it is written by AI and can be wrong, and the feature is in beta.
